How we find undiagnosed CKD — end to end

Chronic kidney disease is often undocumented or mis-staged in Epic. Here's the whole pipeline, from governed data to a clinician-approved write-back — click a step to see what happens and how

Unify the raw clinical signals — and govern every column.

Everything downstream reads one governed source, so the app, dashboards, and Genie always agree.

  • A Lakeflow medallion pipeline lands raw feeds in bronze, cleans and types them in silver, and rolls them up to a patient-level gold table.
  • Sources: outpatient eGFR / creatinine / albuminuria, medications, free-text clinical notes, and the Epic problem-list status.
  • Unity Catalog governs it all — lineage, primary/foreign keys, column comments, and ABAC masking on PHI (MRN shows as MRN-***-****).
Databricks in this step
Unity Catalog — governance, lineage, PHI masking Lakeflow — bronze → silver → gold Delta Lake — the governed tables

Stage every patient from their labs — and flag the gaps, with confidence.

Rules do the identifying; the model and the notes add decision support, not a black-box diagnosis.

  • KDIGO stage is computed from each patient's mean outpatient eGFR (G1 ≥90 → G5 <15), excluding inpatient labs.
  • That lab-derived stage is compared to what's documented in Epic → flags for undocumented and possibly mis-staged CKD.
  • An MLflow model suggests the stage + a confidence score; it's registered in Unity Catalog, served on Model Serving, and batch-scored into gold.
  • AI Functions (ai_query) read the free-text notes to catch nephrology involvement the structured data misses.
Databricks in this step
AI Functions — read clinical notes MLflow + Model Serving — CKD stage model Metric Views — governed KPI rollups

Put the right patients in front of a clinician, with the evidence to decide.

This is what replaces manual chart review — the model did the first pass; the clinician makes the call.

  • A Databricks App reads the governed gold table through a SQL warehouse (the app's service principal — no data leaves governance).
  • Pick a cohort — undocumented, high-risk without nephrology, rapid eGFR decline — and open a patient to see the eGFR trend, lab-derived stage, model suggestion + confidence, meds, and nephrology status.
  • An embedded Genie agent answers plain-English follow-ups on the same governed data, so its numbers match the worklist.
Databricks in this step
Databricks Apps — the clinician worklist Genie — plain-English Q&A AI/BI Dashboards — population view

Record the clinician's confirmed stage — and route it back to Epic, safely.

Nothing auto-writes. A clinician approves every change, and every action is audited.

  • On Approve, the app writes one row to a governed write-back queue (Lakebase / Delta): patient, confirmed stage, referral flag, reviewer, timestamp.
  • In production, a Databricks Job drains that queue into Epic's problem list via FHIR (a Condition resource) or HL7 through the integration layer.
  • The clinician stays in the loop on both ends; the queue is fully audited in Unity Catalog.
