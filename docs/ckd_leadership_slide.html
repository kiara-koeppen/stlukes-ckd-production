Finding undiagnosed CKD, before it's missed

Chronic kidney disease is often undocumented or mis-staged in Epic — finding it today means manual chart review across thousands of patients. This surfaces those patients automatically and closes the gap, with a clinician always in the loop.

1

Governed data

Outpatient labs, medications, clinical notes & the Epic problem list — unified and governed in Unity Catalog.

→
2

Identify & suggest

KDIGO stage from lab trends, an ML model that suggests the stage + confidence, and AI that reads the notes for nephrology context.

→
3

Clinician worklist

A ranked review list in a Databricks App. The clinician opens a patient, sees the evidence, and confirms or overrides the stage.

→
4

Write back to Epic

Approval lands in a governed, human-in-the-loop queue — drained to Epic's problem list via FHIR / HL7 in production.
